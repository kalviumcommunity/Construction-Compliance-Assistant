# DIVISION 23 07 13 - DUCT INSULATION AND ACOUSTIC LININGS

## PART 1 - GENERAL
### 1.01 SUMMARY
A. Section Includes: Internal fiberglass and elastomeric acoustic liners for supply and return air ductwork, transfer ducts, and drop-ceiling return air plenums.

### 1.02 REGULATORY AND CODE COMPLIANCE
A. ASTM E84 / UL 723: Surface Burning Characteristics of Building Materials. All internal duct liners, thermal wrap, adhesives, and acoustic sound-attenuation blankets installed within air distribution ducts or environmental air return plenums shall possess:
1. Flame-Spread Index: Not exceeding 25.
2. Smoke-Developed Index: Not exceeding 50.
B. NFPA 90A: Standard for the Installation of Air-Conditioning and Ventilating Systems.
C. ASTM C1338: Standard Test Method for Determining Fungi Resistance of Insulation Materials and Facings.

## PART 2 - PRODUCTS
### 2.01 ACOUSTIC DUCT LINER
A. Materials: Flexible, mat-faced fiberglass acoustic liner with EPA-registered antimicrobial agent integrated into the airstream surface coating.
B. Thickness: 1.0 inch (25 mm) nominal thickness for standard low-pressure distribution ducts; 2.0 inches (50 mm) for mechanical equipment rooms and rooftop unit connections within 20 feet of fan discharge.
C. Maximum Air Velocity Rating: Rated for continuous velocities up to 5,000 feet per minute (25.4 m/s) with zero fiber erosion per ASTM C1071.

## PART 3 - EXECUTION
### 3.01 INSTALLATION
A. Secure acoustic liner to duct interior using 100% coverage of water-based non-flammable adhesive meeting ASTM C916.
B. In addition to adhesive, fasten liner with mechanical weld pins spaced no greater than 12 inches on center in all directions.
C. All transverse leading edges exposed to the airstream shall be coated with heavy mastic and encapsulated with sheet metal foil edges to prevent delamination and airstream contamination.
