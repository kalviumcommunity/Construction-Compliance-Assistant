# CALIFORNIA ENERGY CODE (TITLE 24, PART 6, SUBCHAPTER 3)

## Section 130.1 - Mandatory Indoor Lighting Controls

Nonresidential, high-rise residential, and hotel/motel buildings shall comply with the applicable requirements of Subsections 130.1(A) through 130.1(F).

### Section 130.1(A) Area Controls
1. Manual Area Control: Each area enclosed by ceiling-height partitions shall provide lighting controls that allow the lighting in that area to be manually turned on and off.
2. The control shall be readily accessible and located in the same enclosed area as the lighting it controls.

### Section 130.1(C) Shut-Off Controls
1. Automatic Shut-Off: In addition to manual area controls, all indoor lighting in commercial spaces shall be equipped with separate automatic shut-off controls that automatically extinguish lighting when the space is unoccupied.
2. Permitted automatic shut-off mechanisms:
   a. Occupant sensing controls (occupancy sensors) configured to automatically turn lighting off within 20 minutes of all occupants leaving the space.
   b. Automatic time-switch controls with an accessible manual override switch having a maximum override duration of 2 hours.
3. Offices 250 square feet or smaller, conference rooms of any size, and multipurpose rooms shall be controlled exclusively with occupant sensing controls (occupancy sensors) operating in auto-off or vacancy mode.

### Section 130.1(D) Automatic Daylighting Controls
1. General lighting in daylit zones (skylit daylit zones and primary sidelit daylit zones) shall be controlled independently with automatic daylighting controls.
2. Luminaires in daylit zones shall automatically adjust electrical lighting power down to 35% or lower in response to available natural daylight.

### Section 130.1(E) Demand Responsive Controls
1. Commercial buildings with general lighting loads greater than 4,000 watts shall be capable of automatically reducing lighting power by at least 15% below total installed lighting power in response to a utility demand response signal.
