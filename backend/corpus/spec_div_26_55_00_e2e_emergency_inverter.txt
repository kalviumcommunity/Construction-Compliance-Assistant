SECTION 26 55 00 - SPECIAL EMERGENCY INVERTER LIGHTING SPECIFICATIONS

1.01 SUMMARY
A. Provide central emergency lighting inverter systems for life safety illumination.

2.01 BATTERY BACKUP RUNTIME REQUIREMENTS
A. Emergency inverter battery backup system shall provide 100% rated load capacity for a MINIMUM duration of 90 MINUTES upon primary AC power failure in compliance with NFPA 101.
B. Transfer time from normal utility power to battery emergency mode shall NOT exceed 50 milliseconds.
