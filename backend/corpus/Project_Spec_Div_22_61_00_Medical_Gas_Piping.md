# DIVISION 22 61 00 - HEALTHCARE MEDICAL GAS AND VACUUM SYSTEMS

## PART 1 - GENERAL
### 1.01 SCOPE OF WORK
A. Furnish and install complete medical gas distribution systems including Medical Surgical Vacuum, Medical Air (MA), Oxygen (O2), and Nitrous Oxide (N2O) in conformance with NFPA 99 (Health Care Facilities Code).

### 1.02 QUALITY ASSURANCE & INSTALLER QUALIFICATIONS
A. Medical gas pipe installers shall be qualified and certified in accordance with ASSE Standard 6010.
B. Brazing procedures and brazer performance qualifications shall conform to ASME Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code, Section IX, or AWS B2.2.

## PART 2 - PRODUCTS
### 2.01 PIPING MATERIALS
A. Tubing: Seamless copper tube conforming to ASTM B819, hard-drawn temper, designated Type L (or Type K for underground distribution), factory cleaned, degreased, and capped for oxygen service.
B. Fittings: Wrought copper solder-joint fittings conforming to ASME B16.22, pre-cleaned and bagged for medical gas service.

## PART 3 - EXECUTION
### 3.01 BRAZING REQUIREMENTS
A. Filler Metal: Brazed joints shall be made using BCuP series brazing filler metal (minimum 5% silver, copper-phosphorus alloy) without flux on copper-to-copper joints. Flux is strictly prohibited on all medical gas copper piping except when joining copper to dissimilar metals (brass valves).
B. Continuous Nitrogen Purge: During brazing of all copper joints, an internal continuous purge of oil-free dry nitrogen (minimum purity 99.5%) shall be maintained to prevent the formation of copper oxide scale inside the tubing.
C. Verification of Purge: The oxygen concentration inside the purged pipe during brazing shall not exceed 1.0% (10,000 ppm) as verified by an electronic oxygen analyzer.

### 3.02 PRESSURE TESTING AND CERTIFICATION
A. Initial Pressure Test: Maintain pneumatic pressure of 150 psig with dry nitrogen for a minimum of 24 hours with zero pressure drop, temperature corrected.
B. Cross-Connection Test: Individual gas identification verification shall be performed by an independent third-party ASSE 6030 verifier before system commissioning.
